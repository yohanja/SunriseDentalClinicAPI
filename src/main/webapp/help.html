Help & Instructions

Use your assigned staff username and password to log in. Once logged in, your session stays active until you log out.

Go to New Appointment from the dashboard. Enter the patient's name, address, contact number, and email. Select the treatment type and dentist, then choose a date and time.

If the contact number matches an existing patient, their record is reused automatically — no duplicate entries are created. A confirmation email is sent to the patient upon successful booking.

Go to View Appointment. You can search either by the Appointment Number, or by the patient's Contact Number to see all their appointments.

Go to Generate Bill and enter the Appointment Number. The system automatically calculates the treatment cost plus a fixed consultation fee.

Once generated, click Mark as Paid to confirm payment, then Print Receipt to produce a printable copy for the patient.

Click the logout icon in the top-right corner at any time to safely end your session.
